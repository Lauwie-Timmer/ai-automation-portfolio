## topic: Why practise calls | why
You get better at a conversation by **having it**, not by reading about it. A simulated client gives you cheap repetitions with instant feedback. This step is deliberate practice: three calls, three different personas, each scored.

**How to run each call:**
- Use a **timer: 30 seconds to answer each question.** Real calls move at that speed.
- **Dictate your answers aloud**, using voice-to-text if you can, because speaking is different from typing.
- One call per sitting.
know: Say the two practice rules for the calls
Q: What are the two rules for each practice call?
A: Answer within 30 seconds, and speak the answers aloud (voice-to-text) instead of typing.

## topic: The three personas | personas
1. **A friendly but vague café owner** who says "I want AI for my business." Practise narrowing: ask for one repeated task and its cost.
2. **A sceptical e-commerce manager** who was burned by a freelancer and cares about price. Practise honesty, evidence from your case studies and explaining why a fixed price is fair.
3. **A technical founder** who tests your claims with detailed questions. Practise saying "I haven't done that, here's how I'd check", and not bluffing.

Use the discovery-call structure from p5-1: agenda, problem, impact, current process, constraints, success, next step.
know: Name the three personas and what each practises
Q: What does each persona practise?
A: Café owner: narrowing a vague request. E-commerce manager: honesty, evidence and price. Technical founder: not bluffing under detailed questions.

## topic: Scoring and improving | score
After **12 exchanges** the sparring partner stops and gives a **scorecard out of 10** for: **questions I asked, listening, honesty about what I can do, clarity, and whether I set a next step.** Then it names **the one thing to fix.**

Write that one thing down and carry it into the next call. Put mistakes in `C:\dev\ai-automation-portfolio\mistakes.md`.

Done when you have three calls, each scored, with at least one improvement visible between call 1 and call 3.
