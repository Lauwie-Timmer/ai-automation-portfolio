## topic: What a niche is | niche
A niche is **not a limit**. It's what makes you **easy to remember and refer**. "I help physiotherapy clinics reduce no-shows with reminders and a smart FAQ" is memorable. "I do AI and automation" isn't.

**Formula:** **I help [who] [get result] by [method].** The result is something the client **counts**: hours saved, leads captured, no-shows.

Q: What is the formula for a one-sentence offer?
A: I help [who] [get result] by [method], where the result is something the client counts.
Q: Is a niche a limit?
A: No; it makes you easy to remember and refer.

## topic: Use evidence | evidence
Pick your niche from **evidence**, not from what sounds impressive:
- Which **jobs got replies**?
- Which **calls felt natural**?
- Which **projects you enjoyed**?
- Which of your packages (p5-pkg) sold or got interest?

A leap without evidence ("I think restaurants need this") is a guess; mark it as a guess and test it.
know: List the evidence to use for choosing a niche
Q: Name three kinds of evidence for a niche.
A: Which jobs got replies, which calls felt natural, and which projects I enjoyed (and which packages got interest).

## topic: Choose and try it | choose
1. Write **3 candidate offers** from your evidence, one sentence each.
2. **Pick one to try for a month.**
3. **Update your headline** and the team pitch to match.

Done when you have one sentence offer that matches the evidence and a stranger could repeat it. If you can't decide, the Mapmaker asks you why before it helps; it won't pick for you.
