## topic: What the exam combines | exam
The Phase 5 exam checks three skills, each scored out of 10:
1. **A proposal review:** the Examiner reads your best real proposal against its job post and tells you what it noticed first, what felt generic, and what you couldn't deliver.
2. **A 5-minute discovery call** with a client who gives a vague brief.
3. **A pricing question** for a small automation job: tasks, hours, buffer, price after fees, scope and exclusions.

Then you hear your level. Use your **real proposals**, not new ones.

## topic: How to prepare | prepare
- **Proposals:** problem first, 120 words, one project link, one question, next step (p5-4).
- **Call:** the seven parts, listen more than you talk, no bluffing, no price on the call (p5-1, p5-6).
- **Pricing:** tasks, hours, +50% buffer, rate, gross up for the fee, scope, exclusions, milestones (p5-5).
- **Privacy and AI:** be ready to say which tools you use and what data they see (p5-ai).

Done when you score **80% or more** on the exam, you can run a 15-minute call without freezing, and your proposals are sent.
