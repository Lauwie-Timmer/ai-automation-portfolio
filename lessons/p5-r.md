## topic: Handling the data-privacy concern | privacy
The clinic owner will worry about patient data. An honest, strong answer:
- **Start without patient data.** Appointment reminders and an FAQ about opening hours and treatments don't need patient records.
- **Health data is a special category under GDPR**, so anything involving it needs proper advice and agreement first.
- **Minimise** what is stored, keep it out of prompts, keep the client's accounts as the owner, and say what is logged and for how long.
- **Be honest about limits:** "I haven't built that with patient data, and I wouldn't start there. Here is what I'd do first."
