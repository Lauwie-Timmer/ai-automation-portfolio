## topic: What clients see in ten seconds | skim
Clients skim. They look at, in this order: **photo, headline, the first two lines of the overview, portfolio items, skills, rate.** Make those strong and specific.

- **Headline:** who you help and with what. "n8n and Python automation for small businesses: lead capture, AI inbox sorting, reports" beats "Full-stack developer and AI expert".
- **Overview, first two lines:** the problem you solve and a proof point (a project you built).
- **Portfolio:** real projects you built, each with its 150-word case study (p4-4), a screenshot and, if you made one, a video. One or two is fine at the start; three once Phase 4 is done. Remove anything with real personal data.
- **Skills:** choose the words clients use in job posts (from your market research), such as `n8n`, `Python`, `API integration`, `AI chatbot`, `automation`.

Start from your headline and overview from p0-6 and update them with what you learned and built.

## topic: The honesty rule | honesty
- **No fake reviews, no invented clients and no skills you can't show.**
- Your portfolio is **real projects you built, labelled as projects** ("practice project", "prototype on sample data").
- Every claim must be true. If a client checks it, it should hold.

Honesty is a business asset here. You have no reviews yet, so credibility comes from specific, checkable work, not from big claims.

## topic: Rate, packages and the Rising Talent badge | rate
- **Hourly rate:** the rate you are happy to work for. The goal in this roadmap is **at least 20 euros an hour**. You can also use **fixed-price offers** for small jobs, which is often easier to win at the start; you design those in p5-pkg after learning pricing in p5-5.
- **Rising Talent badge:** Upwork gives it to promising new freelancers who complete their profile and meet its criteria. It raises your visibility in client searches and, per Upwork's page and general sources, gives a one-time batch of free Connects (30 was the figure in the source for this roadmap). Upwork's page blocks automatic reading, so **open Upwork's own Rising Talent page yourself**, write down the current criteria, and tick off what you already meet.
