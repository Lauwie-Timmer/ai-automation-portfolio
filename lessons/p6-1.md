## topic: Most trouble is communication | comms
Most freelance trouble is **communication, not code**. Clients are happy when they know what is happening. Use your templates from p6-tpl for the kickoff, update messages and handover.

**Before you start:** confirm the **scope in writing**, what you need from the client, the **first milestone** and the **date**.
**During:** send a **short update every 2 to 3 days**, even if it's "no change, testing today".
**If you're stuck or late:** **say so early and give a new date.**

## topic: Delivering and the review | deliver
**Before you deliver:**
1. **Test with the client's sample data.**
2. Write a **short handover**: how it works, how to fix common problems, who to contact.
3. Record a **3-minute video walk-through.**

**At the end:** ask for the review **kindly, and only after the work is accepted.** **Never offer anything in exchange for a review.**

**Payment protection reminder (check the Help Center for current rules):** on **fixed price** the client funds a milestone first (escrow) and you deliver once funded. On **hourly**, protection generally depends on tracking time with **Upwork's time tracker.**

## topic: The delivery checklist | checklist
Make `delivery-checklist.md` from the points above, and send your first update **at the time you promised.** A practical shape:

- [ ] Scope confirmed in writing; kickoff done
- [ ] First milestone funded (or the agreed payment step)
- [ ] Update sent on the promised date
- [ ] Tested with the client's sample data
- [ ] Handover written; video recorded; logins listed but not the secrets
- [ ] Accepted, paid, then review requested

Done when the job is accepted and paid and the client has left a review.
