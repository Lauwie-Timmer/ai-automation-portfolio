## topic: Reusing a job safely | reuse
Every finished job is portfolio material, but it belongs partly to the client.
- **Ask the client for permission** before you describe a project.
- **Remove their name, their data and anything that identifies them** unless they agree.
- Use the **same five parts as p4-4**: problem, what I built, tools, result, what I'd do next. Add the **real result** and, if the client allows it, a **one-line quote** from them.

**Write it within a week of the job ending**, while you remember the details, from your notes only: don't add anything you didn't write down.

## topic: Checking the draft | check
Give your notes to the Clerk and Checker. It turns the notes into the five-part structure **without adding anything you didn't write**, then marks anything that might **identify the client** or that **you can't prove.**

Done when a new case study is on your profile, with the client's agreement.
