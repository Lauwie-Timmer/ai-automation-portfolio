## topic: The cost formula | formula
**Cost per call** = (input tokens × input price + output tokens × output price) ÷ 1,000,000, with prices given **per million tokens**. Output tokens usually cost more than input tokens.

A rule of thumb for English is **4 characters per token** (Dutch and code use more).

Worked example with **illustrative** prices (the real ones are in the next topic): $0.30 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output tokens. A call with 100 input tokens and 30 output tokens costs:

```
input:  100 × 0.30 / 1,000,000 = 0.00003
output:  30 × 2.50 / 1,000,000 = 0.000075
total                          = 0.000105  dollars per call
```

Multiply by calls per day and by 30 for a month. Always do it **per day first**, then per month, and keep the units visible.

Q: A call uses 200 input and 50 output tokens at $0.30 and $2.50 per million. What does it cost?
A: Input 200 × 0.30 / 1,000,000 = $0.00006; output 50 × 2.50 / 1,000,000 = $0.000125; total $0.000185.
Q: Which costs more, input or output tokens, usually?
A: Output tokens.

## topic: Models, prices and where the lever is | models-prices
**Cheap, fast models** are fine for classification and extraction. Use a **larger model** only where quality needs it. Choosing the model is the lever for keeping a client's bill low.

On the Gemini pricing page on 2026-10-05 (standard paid tier, per 1M tokens, text): **Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite** $0.30 input / $2.50 output; **Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview** $2.00 input / $12.00 output (for prompts up to 200k tokens). **Prices change often: always open the provider's pricing page on the day you quote.**

Also note: the free tier is free of charge but uses your content to improve Google's products. For **EEA, Swiss and UK users** you may use **only paid services** when you make an API client available to them. So real client work runs on a **paid account, ideally the client's own**.

Example: 200 messages a day, 100 tokens in and 30 out each = 20,000 input and 6,000 output tokens a day. On Flash-Lite: 0.02 × 0.30 + 0.006 × 2.50 = $0.021 a day, about $0.63 a month. On Pro Preview: 0.02 × 2 + 0.006 × 12 = $0.112 a day, about $3.36 a month.

## topic: Quoting the three costs | quoting
In a quote always separate **three** things:
1. **Your build fee**: once.
2. **The AI usage**: on the client's own paid API account, billed to them by the provider.
3. **Hosting or maintenance**: monthly, if you provide it.

Why separate them: the client sees what they pay you, what the AI costs (and that it scales with use), and what keeps it running. It prevents arguments when usage grows.

**Explaining without jargon**, for example: "The AI charges by the amount of text it reads and writes. For your 500 messages a day that comes to a couple of euros a month, and you pay the AI company directly, so you can always see what it costs."
