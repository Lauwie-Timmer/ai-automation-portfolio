## topic: Why reach out directly | why
Upwork gives you few free Connects a month and every proposal competes with many others, so waiting can be slow. **Direct outreach** to people who know you, or to local businesses, can lead to a first paid job and a first review sooner.

Do this **after you have at least one case study** (p4-4), so you have proof to show.

## topic: Who to contact and what to offer | who
- **Start warm:** family, friends, friends' parents, your gym, your mother's physiotherapy clinic, your own app. Warm contacts say yes to a 15-minute conversation far more often than strangers.
- **Pick problems you can already solve:** missed leads, slow replies, manual copy and paste between tools, weekly reports done by hand.
- **The physiotherapy clinic:** build only around **non-patient information** at first: appointment-request intake, an FAQ about opening hours and treatments, review requests. **No patient records**, because health data is a special category under GDPR.

Write a list of **10**: 5 warm and 5 local small businesses you can walk into or phone. For each, note a likely problem.

## topic: The message | message
**Structure:**
1. **Who you are in one line.**
2. **The specific problem** you noticed or heard about.
3. **One result you built** (your case study).
4. **One low-effort ask:** "Could I ask you three questions about how you handle X? 15 minutes."
No attachments and no long pitch.

Write three versions: **warm**, **local business** and **LinkedIn**.

**Rules:**
- Be **honest that this is early work.** Never pretend to have clients you don't have.
- **EU rules on unsolicited marketing are strict**, especially emails to individuals and sole traders. Prefer **in-person visits, phone calls, LinkedIn messages and warm introductions.** Ask your accountant or a professional before emailing strangers in bulk.

## topic: The first paid pilot | pilot
If someone says yes:
- Agree a **small fixed scope in writing**: half a page with the goal, what you'll deliver, what isn't included, the price, the dates, and what you need from them (full templates come in p6-tpl).
- A **fair price**: a reduced price is fine, **free is not**, because free work has no boundaries.
- **Permission to use it as a case study** afterwards.

**Track it** in a sheet: who, when, reaction, next step. Reach out to 5, **book at least 2 conversations**, and after each write down what you learned.

Done when ten contacts are listed, five reached, at least two conversations held and noted, and you can say what you'd offer each one.
