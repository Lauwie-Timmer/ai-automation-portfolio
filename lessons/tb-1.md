## topic: What ASO is | aso
**App Store Optimization (ASO)** is how people **find your app in the store**. Two things decide the result: **search** (do you show up for the words people type) and **conversion** (do they install once they see you).

- **Searched fields:** the **title**, **subtitle** and the hidden **keyword field** on iOS; the **title**, **short description** and **full description** on Google Play. (Character limits change, so check Apple's and Google's current guidance before you write.)
- **Conversion:** the **screenshots** (the first two matter most), the **icon** and the **ratings** decide whether people install.

A: How people find your app in the store. The title, subtitle and keyword field on iOS, and the title, short description and description on Google Play.

A: The screenshots (especially the first two), the icon and the ratings.

## topic: Choosing keywords | keywords
Choose keywords by **what people search for**, not by what you call things. Start with the words in the **problem**: "workout tracker", "macro tracker", "food diary".

**Method:**
1. List **20 candidate keywords.**
2. **Group them by intent** (what is the person trying to do?).
3. Judge each: is it **too competitive** (everyone uses it), **too vague** ("health") or **not what people search** (your own jargon)?
4. Pick the **top 8** for the title, subtitle and keyword field, and write **a reason for every word.**

**Test one change at a time** and wait long enough to see a difference, otherwise you can't tell what worked.

## topic: Doing tb-1 | do
After your app is approved: write the 20 candidate keywords with reasons, pick the top 8, draft the title, subtitle, keywords and description, and have Claude review the listing **as a store user** (it challenges each choice and doesn't rewrite).

Done when every keyword has a reason and the first two screenshots show the main benefit.
