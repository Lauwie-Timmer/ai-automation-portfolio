## topic: Kickoff questions and scope of work | start
**Kickoff questionnaire:**
- What problem are we solving, and what does success look like?
- Which tools and accounts are involved, and **who owns them**?
- What data is involved, and is any of it **personal or sensitive**?
- Who approves, and who is the contact?
- Deadline and any fixed dates?
- What is out of scope?

**Scope of work:**
- **Goal** in one sentence.
- **Deliverables** as a list.
- **What is not included** (exclusions).
- **What you need from the client**, and by when.
- **Milestones with prices.**
- **How many rounds of changes** are included.
- **How acceptance works** (who says "done", and how).
- **How AI tools and client data are handled** (the consent sentence from p5-ai).

## topic: Change request, handover and update message | run
**Change request** (handles scope creep politely): what is being asked; what it changes in **time and price**; and a **yes or no from the client before you start**.

**Handover document:** what was built; how to run and check it; **where the logins live (never the secrets themselves)**; what to do when it breaks; who to contact; a link to the **video walk-through**.

**Update message (four lines):**
1. Done since last time.
2. Next steps.
3. Anything blocked.
4. The date of the next update.
