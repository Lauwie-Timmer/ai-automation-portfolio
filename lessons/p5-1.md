## topic: What a discovery call is | what
A **discovery call** is the first conversation with a client **before any price or proposal**. Its job is for **you to understand their problem** and for **them to see that you listen**. It is not a pitch.

You have sales experience from door-to-door solar, and the situation is different:
- **Door-to-door:** you persuade a stranger who didn't ask.
- **Here:** the client already posted a job, so they want help. You are **qualifying each other**: you decide whether you can help and whether the job is a good one; they decide whether to trust you.

Goal of this step: run a **15-minute call** where you ask good questions and **the client does most of the talking**.

## topic: The seven parts of a call | seven
Use this structure:
1. **Greet and set the agenda:** "I'll ask a few questions, then tell you how I'd approach it, then we agree on next steps."
2. **The problem:** what is happening now, and what do you want instead?
3. **The impact:** what does it cost you in time or money?
4. **The current process and tools:** how is it done today, with what?
5. **Constraints:** data, access, budget range, deadline.
6. **Success:** how will we know it worked?
7. **Next step:** "I'll send a written proposal within 24 hours."

Write your own list of **8 discovery questions** in `discovery-questions.md`, in your own words, covering parts 2 to 6. Plus a **3-sentence opening** about who you are and what you do; say it aloud twice.

## topic: Listening and not bluffing | listening
- **Listen more than you talk.** After an answer, ask "can you tell me more?" or repeat their last few words as a question. **Silence is fine**; clients fill it with the useful part.
- **Never promise what you can't do.** "I haven't done exactly that, but I've built X, and here's how I'd check it works" is stronger than bluffing.
- **A vague client** ("I want AI for my business") needs narrowing: "What is one task your team repeats every week?" Then ask what it costs them.
- **Don't quote a price on the call** unless the scope is clear. Say: "I'll send a fixed price with what's included."
