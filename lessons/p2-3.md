## topic: What OAuth is and why | oauth
**OAuth** lets n8n act in your Google account **without knowing your password**. Instead you give n8n a limited permission, and you can take it back any time.

The people involved:
- **You** (the account owner)
- **Google**, which holds your data
- **n8n**, the app that wants access
- the **app registration** in Google Cloud: because you self-host n8n, **you create the app** that stands for n8n

How it works in short: Google gives your app a **Client ID** and a **Client Secret**. You paste them into an n8n credential. Then you click **Sign in with Google** once, log in, and approve the access. Google gives n8n a **token**, and n8n uses the token from then on.

Why it's worth the effort: clients ask for Google Sheets and Gmail constantly, and every one of those needs this. The first time takes 30 to 45 minutes; later ones take minutes.

An alternative for email only is SMTP with a Gmail **app password**. OAuth is the skill to learn.

Q: Why OAuth instead of giving n8n your Google password?
A: n8n gets a limited, revocable permission (a token) and never learns my password.

## topic: Setting up the Google Cloud app | google-cloud
Follow the n8n docs page *Google credentials* (custom OAuth2 for self-hosted). The steps, in order:

1. **Create a project** in Google Cloud Console (project dropdown, New project).
2. **Enable the APIs**: APIs & Services, Library: enable the **Google Sheets API** and the **Gmail API**. (Sheets also needs the Google Drive API.)
3. **OAuth consent screen**: choose **External** as the audience. A new External app starts in **Testing** mode. Add your own Gmail address as a **Test user**.
4. **Create the credentials**: APIs & Services, Credentials, Create credentials, **OAuth client ID**, type **Web application**.
5. In **Authorized redirect URIs** paste the **redirect URL** that n8n shows on its credential screen. On your setup it looks like `http://localhost:5678/rest/oauth2-credential/callback`.
6. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** from Google into the n8n credential and click **Sign in with Google**.

The **redirect URL** is where Google sends you back after you approve. It must match n8n's **exactly**, character for character. A mismatch is the most common error ("redirect_uri_mismatch").

**Testing mode:** consent and tokens expire after about **7 days**, so you will have to reconnect the credential. That is normal for Testing apps.

> Warning: never paste a Client ID or Client Secret into chat or into your repo. They go only into the n8n credential.

## topic: The sheet and the nodes | sheets-gmail
1. Create a Google Sheet called `Signups` with the headers `name`, `email`, `received_at` in **row 1**. The header names matter: n8n maps your fields to columns by name.
2. In `04 signup intake`, on the **true** branch (after Edit Fields, before Respond to Webhook) add:
   - a **Google Sheets** node, operation **Append Row**, choosing your Google credential, the `Signups` sheet, and mapping `name`, `email`, and `received_at` (expression `{{ $now }}`)
   - a **Gmail** node, operation **Send a message**, with the sender's email as the recipient (for testing, use **your own** email), a subject and a short confirmation text
3. Use the **credentials dropdown** in both nodes. One Google Cloud project with both APIs enabled can serve both, though each node can also have its own credential.
4. Run the **valid** test case from Python, with the secret header from p2-sec. Check that a row appeared and an email arrived.

> Warning: while testing send emails only to yourself. A loop or a bug that emails real people is hard to undo.
