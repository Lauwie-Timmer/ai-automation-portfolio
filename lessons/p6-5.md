## topic: Why agree rules early | why
Friendships survive money better when the rules are **agreed early and written down**. It's much easier **before** there is any money. This step is a meeting, not a task you do alone: a 90-minute conversation with your three friends, ending in a **one-page written agreement** that everyone has read.
know: Say why to agree the rules before any money exists
Q: Why agree the rules early?
A: It's much easier before there's any money, and written rules protect the friendship.

## topic: What to decide | decide
- **Roles:** who sells, who builds, who handles operations and invoicing.
- **How work is divided** between the four of you.
- **How money is split** and **when it's paid out.**
- **What happens if someone leaves.**
- **Loose team or registered company?** Ask an accountant before forming a company.
- **How to review each other's work** (p6-git).

**Skills to use:** mathematics for data and reporting, computer science for engineering, business administration (technische bedrijfskunde) for processes and pricing, and mechanical engineering for technical or industrial clients' processes.

**Tools and access:** use the shared GitHub repo and your case studies as the team portfolio. Give each other access to a shared n8n and a shared password manager, **never over chat.**

Q: List five decisions the team agreement covers.

Q: How do you share logins and keys in a team?

## topic: The meeting | meeting
1. **Send the team the roadmap and dashboard** so each person can follow it.
2. Run the meeting with a **5-point agenda**: **skills, roles, money split, first target client type, how to review each other's work.**
3. Write the decisions in `team-agreement.md`, one page.

If you want help preparing, the Interviewer asks 6 questions about your friends, their skills and time, then drafts the agenda and the list of decisions with short explanations of each option. **It doesn't decide for you.**

Done when a written one-page agreement exists that everyone has read.
