## topic: Read the market, don't guess | read-market
Don't guess your niche. Read **real job posts** and look for **repeated problems**, **budgets**, and the **wording clients use**. Their wording is your future headline.

A good **first job** is: small, clearly described, from a client with a **verified payment method** and some history, and fits something you've already built.

Don't apply yet unless a job matches something you've built. Proposals come in Phase 5, and you can start as soon as Project B in Phase 4 is done.
know: Say what makes a good first job
Q: What makes a good first Upwork job for you?
A: Small, clearly described, a client with a verified payment method and some history, and it fits something I've built.

## topic: Red flags and scams | red-flags
Spot these before you waste time or lose money:
- asks for **free work** or a "test project"
- wants to move to **WhatsApp or Telegram**, or pay **outside Upwork**
- asks you to **pay a fee, buy tools or buy crypto first**
- an "**AI training**" job that needs a fee
- asks for **ID photos or a selfie**
- sends **unknown files**
- a **vague scope with a big promise**
- **no payment method verified**

You'll learn the full list in p5-3. Never click unknown files or links from a job post, and never move off the platform.

Q: Name six red flags in a job post.
A: Free work or a test project, moving to WhatsApp/Telegram or paying outside Upwork, asking me to pay or buy something first, an AI-training job needing a fee, asking for ID or a selfie, unknown files, a vague scope with a big promise, or no verified payment method.

## topic: Doing the research | research
1. Browse Upwork for jobs matching `n8n`, `automation`, `Make.com`, `Python API`, `chatbot`, `AI agent` and `Zapier`. Open **15 job posts**.
2. Make `market-research.md` with a table: **title**, **what the client wants**, **tools named**, **budget**, **client history**, and **could I do it after Phase 4** (yes, no, partly).
3. Find the **3 most repeated needs**. Write one sentence for each **in the client's own words**.
4. Run the prompt on the dashboard: Claude interviews you about what you'd enjoy, then helps you decide which need to build your portfolio around.

Done when you have a table of 15 jobs and three repeated needs, with a plan for which one Project C should serve.
