## topic: Why a demo video | why
Clients often **watch the video and skim the text**. A short demo shows you can do the work **and** communicate. This step is a bonus, so do it when the three projects are done.

**Keep each demo to about 2 minutes with three parts:**
1. The **problem** in one sentence.
2. The **result running** (the workflow firing, the report appearing, the chat answering).
3. **One thing you'd improve.**

Speak to a non-technical person. Say what happens, not which node you clicked.

A: The problem in one sentence, the result running, and one thing I'd improve.

## topic: Recording and removing secrets | record
Any screen recorder works. On Windows, **Game Bar** records the active window: press **Win+G**, then use the capture widget's record button (or **Win+Alt+R** to start and stop).

**Before you share, check the whole recording for secrets**, frame by frame at the risky moments:
- API keys, tokens and passwords (n8n credential screens, `.env` files, Supabase's API Keys page),
- real email addresses and names, customer data, and browser tabs and notifications showing private things.

Use fake data for the demo itself. If anything sensitive appears, re-record or cut it; blurring a video you've already shared is too late.
