## topic: Reading a post: problem, deliverable, signals | read
Read every post for three things:
1. **The problem:** what hurts? Often it's buried: "we lose leads" is the real problem behind "need a CRM integration".
2. **The deliverable:** what the client *thinks* they need. It may differ from the problem; your job is to connect them.
3. **The signals:** budget, urgency, tools named, how clearly it's written, the client's history.

Write each of the three in one line per post. If you can't state the problem in your own words, the post is too vague or you haven't read it properly.
know: Name the three things to read for in a job post
Q: What are the three things to read for?
A: The problem (what hurts), the deliverable (what they think they need) and the signals (budget, urgency, tools, clarity).
Q: How can the problem differ from the deliverable?
A: A client may ask for a CRM integration (deliverable) when the real problem is that leads get lost.

## topic: Checking the client | client
Look at the client's profile before applying:
- **Payment method verified** (a new client without one is higher risk),
- **total spent** and **hire rate** (how many posts end in a hire),
- **reviews from freelancers**, and how long they've been on Upwork,
- how many **proposals** the job already has.

None of these alone decides it; together they tell you whether the client is real and pays.
know: List what to check on a client's profile
Q: Name four things to check on a client before applying.
A: Payment verified, total spent, hire rate, reviews from freelancers (and time on Upwork).

## topic: Connects and cost | connects
Each proposal costs **Connects**. Figures below are from Upwork and third-party summaries in October 2026; **Upwork changes them, so check your own account**:
- A Connect costs **$0.15**, in bundles of at least 10.
- Free accounts get **10 Connects a month**; the Freelancer Plus plan (**$19.99 a month**) includes **100** (newer sources say 100, older ones say 60: read the plan page in your own account before paying).
- The Rising Talent badge, if you get it, can add a one-time batch of free Connects.
- The job post shows **how many Connects a proposal costs** and how many proposals it already has. Costs differ per job, so a couple of proposals can use up a free month.

So **apply only to strong matches.** Don't spend Connects on jobs with red flags.

## topic: Red flags and scams | scams
Walk away or report when a client:
- asks for **free samples** or a "test project";
- wants to move to **WhatsApp or Telegram**, or to **pay outside Upwork**;
- asks you to **pay a fee, buy software or buy crypto** first;
- asks you to "verify" a **crypto wallet**;
- offers a quick "**AI training**" job and then wants a fee to unlock the tool;
- asks for a **photo of your ID or a selfie** "for compliance";
- sends **unknown files or links to download**;
- makes **a huge promise with a tiny budget**;
- writes **nothing about what they want**.

**Rules:** never download unknown files, never pay to get work, never leave the platform to be paid, and **report anything suspicious to Upwork**. Upwork's "how to identify and report scams" page lists current patterns.

A: Free samples or a test project; moving to WhatsApp/Telegram or paying outside Upwork; paying a fee or buying software/crypto first; wallet verification; ID or selfie requests; unknown files or links; huge promise with tiny budget; no description of what they want.

A: Download unknown files, pay to get work, leave the platform to be paid, or ignore something suspicious instead of reporting it.

## topic: Scoring and the exercise | score
**Score each job 1 to 5 on fit, and apply only if it's 4 or higher.** Fit means: do you understand the problem, have you built something similar, is the budget reasonable, is the client credible, and are there no red flags?

Exercise: take **5 posts from your market research** and write the problem, deliverable, signals, red flags and a fit score for each. Remove client names before pasting them to Claude. Done when Claude agrees with at least 4 of your 5 assessments and you can name the red flags without looking.
know: Say the apply threshold and what fit means
Q: What score means you should apply and what does fit include?
A: 4 or higher out of 5. Fit includes understanding the problem, a similar build, a reasonable budget, a credible client and no red flags.
