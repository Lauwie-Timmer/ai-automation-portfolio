## topic: Why the first week matters | week1
The **first week** decides retention: most people who don't come back in the first days never do. So prepare three things before launch:
1. A **launch-day message** to waitlist users: clear, short, with the link and what to do first.
2. **One simple retention number**, for example "people who log a second workout within a week".
3. A **rating prompt** at a **happy moment** (right after something good, such as finishing a workout), **not at first open**.
