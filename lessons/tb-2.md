## topic: One channel, one hypothesis | channel
Don't try everything. Pick **one channel** (for example short videos, a fitness community, a small paid test) and **measure** it: **installs per hour of your time or per euro spent.**

**Write the hypothesis first**, before you start: "I expect about **X installs** from **Y**." Writing it first stops you from explaining the result away afterwards.

Choose the **measure** too (installs, signups, or people who log a second workout), and decide **what result would make you stop.**

Q: Why test only one channel?
A: Trying everything gives you no clear result; one channel measured properly tells you what works.
Q: What is the form of the hypothesis?
A: "I expect about X installs from Y", written before the test starts.

## topic: Run it and decide | decide
1. Write the hypothesis and the measure in `acquisition-test.md`.
2. Run it for **2 weeks**.
3. Write the **result**, compare it with the hypothesis, and decide: **continue, change or stop.**

Done when there is a written result with a decision. If you can't say what would make you stop, you haven't designed a test yet.
