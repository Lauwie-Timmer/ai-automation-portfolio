What is the goal of a discovery call?	For you to understand the client's problem, and for them to see you listen. It is not a pitch.
The seven parts of a discovery call?	Agenda, the problem, the impact, current process and tools, constraints, what success looks like, and the next step.
Why not quote a price on the first call?	The scope isn't clear yet. Say you'll send a written fixed price with what's included.
What do you do when you don't know an answer on a call?	Say so, say what you have built that is close, and say how you'd check. Never bluff.
How do you get a client to say more?	Ask "can you tell me more?" or repeat their last few words as a question, and accept silence.
What does one Connect cost, and how many are free?	$0.15 each. Basic accounts get 10 free a month.
What does the Rising Talent badge give?	More visibility and a one-time 30 free Connects.
Name four scam or red flag signs on Upwork.	Pay a fee first, move to WhatsApp or Telegram, ID photos or selfies, unknown file downloads, test projects, crypto.
How do you score a job before applying?	1 to 5 on fit, and apply only to 4 or higher.
Proposal structure?	Their problem in your words, your approach, one relevant project, one clarifying question, and a next step. About 120 words.
How do you estimate a fixed price?	List tasks, estimate hours, add about 50% buffer, multiply by your rate, and add the platform fee.
What goes in a scope?	What's included, what isn't, rounds of changes, what you need from the client and when. Anything outside is a new quote.
Why use milestones?	They protect both sides, and you don't start work on a milestone that isn't funded.
Hourly vs fixed protection on Upwork?	Fixed price uses funded milestones (escrow). Hourly protection generally depends on tracking time with Upwork's own tracker. Check the Help Center.
Roughly what is Upwork's fee now?	Variable, around 10% for most freelancers, and shown before you apply.
What does a package contain?	Name, who it is for, what's included, what isn't, delivery time, price, and what you need from the client.
What goes in your AI-use policy?	Tools you use, what data each sees, what you never send, how you mask personal data, and a consent sentence for clients.

Why start outreach with warm contacts?	They agree to a 15-minute talk far more often than strangers.
Is it OK to do a first pilot for free?	No. A reduced price is fine, but free work has no boundaries. Agree scope, price and case-study permission in writing.
Why be careful emailing strangers in the EU?	Unsolicited marketing rules are strict, especially for individuals and sole traders. Prefer visits, calls and warm introductions.
